---
title: Raspberry cheesecake
description: An oven-baked cheescake, highly addictive, beware.
category: dessert
ingredients:
  - 340g raspberries
  - 50g granulated sugar
  - 60ml water
  - 1 tbsp lemonjuice
  - 170g graham crackers
  - 100g unsalted butter
  - 680g cream cheese
  - 120g sour cream
  - 3 large eggs
servings: 12
prepTime: 45 minutes
cookTime: 50 minutes
---

import { Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

## Instructions

<Steps>
1. Combine your raspberries along with the sugar, lemon juice and water over low to medium heat.

2. Let it simmer until the sauce thickens.

3. Melt the butter in the microwave for 30s.

4. Crush your graham crackers into powder and mix the melted butter into it. Spread and compact homogeneously over the sprinform base.

5. Bake for 15 min at 180C in the oven. Take out and leave the springform pan to cool down.

6. Mix the cream cheese and sour cream. Avoid clumps.

7. When smooth, add the lightly beaten eggs to the mixture and mix until smooth.

</Steps>

## Tips

1. Keep the cheese at room temperature for easier mixing.

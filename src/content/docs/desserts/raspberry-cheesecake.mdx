---
title: Raspberry cheesecake
description: An oven-baked cheescake, highly addictive, beware.
category: dessert
ingredients:
  - 340g raspberries
  - 50g granulated sugar
  - 60ml water
  - 1 tbsp lemonjuice
  - 170g graham crackers
  - 100g unsalted butter
  - 680g cream cheese
  - 120g sour cream
  - 3 large eggs
servings: 12
prepTime: 45 minutes
cookTime: 50 minutes
---

import { Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

## Instructions

<Steps>
1. First step: sauce

- Combine your raspberries along with the sugar, lemon juice and water over low to medium heat.
- Let it simmer until the sauce thickens.

2. Second step: base

- Melt the butter in the microwave for 30s.
- Crush your graham crackers into powder and mix the melted butter into it. Spread and compact homogeneously over the sprinform base.
- Bake for 15 min at 180C in the oven. Take out and leave the springform pan to cool down.

3. Third step: cheese filling

- Mix the cream cheese and sour cream. Avoid clumps.
- When smooth, add the lightly beaten eggs to the mixture and mix until smooth.

</Steps>

## Tips

- Keep the cheese at room temperature for easier mixing.

---
title: Marzipan gelatine
description: Lactose-based gelatine with peanut candy
category: Dessert
ingredients:
  - 1 can of sweetened condensed milk (approx. 387 g)
  - 1 can of evaporated milk (360–410 ml)
  - 1 large package of cream cheese (approx. 300 g)
  - 8 mazapan units (mexican peanut candy, 28 g each)
  - 1 box of 4 sachets of unflavoured gelatine (28 g total)
servings: 6-8
prepTime: 20 minutes
cookTime: 120 minutes
---

import { Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

## Instructions

<Steps>

1. Prepare the mould - Lightly grease the mould with oil and place it in the refrigerator while you prepare the other ingredients, for about 20 minutes.

2. Hydrate the gelatine - Mix the gelatine with ¾ cup (about 180 ml) of water. Let it hydrate. Then dissolve it using a bain-marie (water bath). Alternatively, microwave it in 15-second intervals, stirring between each interval, until completely dissolved. Do not let it boil.

3. Blend - Place all the ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth. Add the dissolved gelatine last and blend briefly until fully incorporated.

4. Refrigerate - Pour the mixture into the prepared mould and refrigerate until completely set.

</Steps>

## Tips

- You can use smashed fruit (strawberries, mango, etc.) instead of mazapan and keep the rest of the ingredients as a base for a milk gelatine of any kind.

---
title: Plum Pie
description: A short introduction to the dish.
category: dessert
ingredients:
  - 200 g flour
  - 1/2 tablespoon salt
  - 50 g grated almonds
  - 75 g butter
  - 1 dl water
  - 1 tablespoon vinegar
  - 750 g plums
  - 1.5 dl milk
  - 1 dl cream
  - 2 eggs
  - 2 tablespoons of corn starch
  - 4 tablespoons of sugar
servings: 8
prepTime: 35 minutes
cookTime: 75 minutes
---

import { Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

## Instructions

<Steps>

1. Dough: Mix flour, salt, and almonds; add butter and rub in until crumbly. Add water and vinegar, bring together to form a dough, do not knead. Wrap in plastic wrap and chill for 30 minutes.

2. Topping: Roll out the dough, line the prepared baking sheet with it, and prick the dough base with a fork. Sprinkle with breadcrumbs or almonds. Place the plums on top.

3. Custard: Briefly mix all ingredients, pour over the fruit. Bake the tart in the lower half of a preheated oven at 200°C (390°F) for 30-35 minutes. Enjoy warm or cooled. Dust with powdered sugar, if desired.

</Steps>

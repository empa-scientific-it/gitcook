---
title: Pancake
description: Everybody loves them, no intro needed.
category: dessert
ingredients:
  - 375 g flour
  - 2 eggs
  - 210 ml milk
  - 1 tablespoon salt
servings: 6
prepTime: 10 minutes
cookTime: 20 minutes
---

import { Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

## Instructions

<Steps>

1. Add all ingredients into a bowl.

2. Mix by hand or mixer

3. Pour a dose onto a pan and fry on both sides

</Steps>

## Tips

- Fill or roll with marmelade or Nutella

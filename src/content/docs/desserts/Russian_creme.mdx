---
title: Russian Creme
description: This delicious dessert is for adults only.
category: dessert
ingredients:
  - 3 egg yolks
  - 60 g sugar
  - 2.5 dl of whipped cream
  - 2 Soup spoons Rhum
servings: 4
prepTime: 10 minutes

---

import { Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

## Instructions

<Steps>

1. Whip the egg yolks with the sugar
2. Add the Rhum
3. Add delicately the whipped cream.

</Steps>

## Tips

- Optional tip or variation: remove Rhum if kids or pregnent women

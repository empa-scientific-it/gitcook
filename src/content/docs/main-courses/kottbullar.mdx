---
title: Kottbullar and potatismos
description: Epic Swede food
category: main-course 
ingredients:
  - One pack of ikea kottbullar
  - 1 kg potatoes
  - Stick of butter
  - Milk
  - Salt and pepper
servings: 4 
prepTime: 5 minutes  
cookTime: 30 minutes 
---

import { Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

## Instructions

<Steps>

1. Boil potatoes

2. Fry Kottbullar

3. Mash potatoes and mix with milk and butter as you like

4. Spice with salt and pepper

</Steps>

## Tips (optional)

- More butter is better!!!

---
title: Gnocchi di patate
description: A simple main course made with potatoes and flour. No sauce is included here.
category: Main course
ingredients:
  - 1 kg of potatoes
  - 300 g of flour
  - 1 egg
  - salt
servings: 4
prepTime: 20 minutes
cookTime: 40 minutes
---

import { Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

## Instructions

<Steps>

1. Boil the potatoes in a large pot for 30-40 minutes.

2. Mash the potatoes while still hot onto the flour. No need to peel them.

3. Knead everything by han until you get a soft but firm dough.

4. Take a part of the dough and roll it out with your fingertips to obtain strips about 3/4 inch thick.

5. Cut the strips into small pieces and drop a few at a time in boiling salted water. As soon as they float, the potato gnocchi are considered cooked and ready to be drained and seasoned.

</Steps>

## Tips

- Add butter and sage for a good seasoning sauce.
- Enjoy!

## Instructions

<Steps>

1. **Peel and slice potatoes** thinly; optionally slice onion.
2. **Cook slowly in plenty of olive oil** with salt until very soft, not crispy.
3. **Drain well** and mix with beaten eggs.
4. **Rest 5 minutes** so the potatoes absorb some egg.
5. Heat a little oil in a non-stick pan and add the mixture.
6. **Cook on medium-low heat** until the bottom is set.
7. Cover with a plate, **flip**, and slide it back into the pan.
8. Cook the other side for **2–4 minutes**, depending on how runny you like the centre.
9. Rest a few minutes and serve.


</Steps>

---
title: Recipe of Shelpek
description: Shelpek is a flat bread from Central Asia. There are several methods of preparing it; this is one of the simplest and it comes from central Kazakhstan.
category: appetizer
ingredients:
  - 400 g of flour
  - 200 ml of water
  - a pinch of salt
  - sunflower oil
servings: 7
prepTime: 20 minutes
cookTime: 15 minutes
---

import { Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

## Instructions

<Steps>

1. Mix flour, water and a pinch of salt in a bowl.
2. Knead the dough and let it rest for 5 minutes or a little longer.

3. Spread some flour on the table and put the rested dough on it.

4. Roll the dough a little bit with your hands and divide it into 7 equal parts.
5. Roll out each of the 7 parts of the dough.
6. Fry the rolled dough in sunflower oil on a medium heat.

</Steps>

## Tips

- Always make 7 pieces of shelpek even if you change the amount of ingredients.

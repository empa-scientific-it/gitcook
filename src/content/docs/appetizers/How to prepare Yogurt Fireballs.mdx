## Instructions

<Steps>

1. Mix Greek yogurt and cottage cheese in a bowl very well.

2. Add chopped onion, bell pepper, chilies, and green coriander to the mix.

3. Take one slice of white bread, trim the edges, moisten the bread with water, and then stuff the mix (1 tablespoon) into the bread slice softly, and seal the bread by pulling the corners.

4. Heat the oil over medium to high heat.

5. Slide the rolls into the oil and fry them till they turn golden brown.

6. Drain the excess oil with a paper towel.

8. Cut them diagonally across the middle and serve with the mint sauce.

</Steps>

## Tips
If you have any trouble with the filling, try using plastic wrap.

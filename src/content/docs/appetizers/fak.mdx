---
title: How to cook french fries
description: A short introduction to the dish.
category: side dishes
ingredients:
  - 2 kilos of potatoes
  - 8 to 10 spoons of oil
servings: 4
prepTime: 10 minutes
cookTime: 20 minutes
---

import { Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

## Instructions

<Steps>

1. First step: Peel the potatoes
2. Second step: Cut into small pieces
3. Third step: Put oil on thre pan
4. Third step: Pour the potatoes in the oil
5. Fourth step: Wait for 5 to 20 minutes of cooking
6. Final step: French fries are ready!!
</Steps>

## Tips

- Add salt either while cooking or after cooking
